# Peer singles, plain-language edition

Written 2026-09-10, replacing the singles in `2026-09-09-peer-posts.md`. Same
facts, same order, same images; the words are for people who do not know what a
TEE is. Threads 1 and 2 are unchanged and live in the older file.

Rules for every single: the link goes in the first reply
(`sable.primecircle.cloud/#who`), one tag per post, one question at the end,
"I hold SABL." last, under 280 characters. Post one per half day in the order of
the table in the older file (Phala first, Tinfoil last).

Plain words used, so the meaning stays exact:

- "sealed-off part of the chip" or "sealed hardware" = a TEE (trusted execution
  environment).
- "hardware proof" = attestation, the chip's own statement that it ran untampered.
- "signed receipt" = the per-response signature (EIP-191, secp256k1) any wallet
  can verify.
- "pay per request" = x402, paying per call straight from a wallet.
