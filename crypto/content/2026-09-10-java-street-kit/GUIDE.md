# Gatekeeper on the street: the guide

One page for the person running the day. Read it twice. Version 10 Sep 2026.

## What you are doing

You are getting people to play a 60-second phone game called Gatekeeper and to put
their score on its leaderboard. The game lives on an independent site about Sable
Network, an AI compute service. The site is run by a private person who holds the
SABL token and is not part of Sable Network.

You are not selling anything. You are not promoting a token. You are running a game
day, and the count of played runs is how the day is measured.

## The one rule

No token. No price. No "invest", "buy", "early", "moon", "SABL". Not on the poster,
not from your mouth, not in your posts.

Why. Indonesia's regulator OJK only allows licensed exchanges to advertise crypto
assets, it summoned influencers in June 2026 for promoting unlicensed platforms, and
its 2026 conduct rule (POJK 6/2026) requires paid promotion to be disclosed and
forbids promises of returns. SABL is not on the OJK list. If you promote it on the
street, the risk is yours, not the founder's. The game is legal and fun. Keep it there.

If someone asks "is this crypto?": "The site is about an AI service that also has a
token. I am not here for the token and I am not allowed to promote it. The game is
free: no wallet, no account, no money."

If someone asks "can I earn money?": "No. It is a game. The best score gets its name
lit on the board, that is all."

## The game, so you can explain it

You are the door of an AI agent. Requests arrive at the door. Sealed ones pass and
become receipts. Broken seals and runaway loops must be refused before they reach
the door. Only refusals build your streak. A wave held without a leak gives budget
back. There is no clock: the run lasts as long as your budget. Runs of 20 seconds or
longer are ranked. The board replays every run from its taps before it counts, so a
faked score never lands.

The Demo button plays a 60-second run by itself. Use it to show a group what the
game looks like before they try.

## The link and the QR

    sable.primecircle.cloud/java

That short link sends the phone to the game with the tag "java" attached (the QR
carries the same destination in full: `sable.primecircle.cloud/?src=java#play`).
Every run that starts from either is counted under "java" on the board. That count
is how the founder measures your day. Use only these: on the poster, in the QR, and
when you type it for someone. The plain site link without the tag counts nothing.

The site loads a small 3D library, so it needs a normal phone browser and mobile
data. It works on Android and iPhone. If a phone is very old, use the Demo.

## Where to go

Places where people have five minutes and a phone: computer science faculties,
coworking spaces, tech meetups, cafes near a university, game cafes, hackathon
venues. Not traffic lights, not places of worship, not schools with minors.

Ask the owner before you put up a poster inside. For posters outside, check your
city's rules; many cities fine wild posting. A hand-held sign is always fine.

## The 20-second pitch

Indonesian (draft, fix the wording so it sounds like you):

    Mau coba game 60 detik? Kamu jadi penjaga pintu buat AI.
    Scan, main, masuk papan peringkat. Gratis, tanpa akun.

English:

    Want a 60-second game? You are the gatekeeper for an AI.
    Scan, play, get on the board. Free, no account.

Then hand them the QR, let them press Play, and step back. When the run ends, ask
them to type a name and, if they have one, their X handle, and press submit. That
is one counted run. Names can be anything; handles are optional.

## Ask for a video

If they liked it, ask them to film their next run (10 to 60 seconds, phone held
upright) and post it on X with **#GatekeeperJava** and **@Sablenetwork**. Everyone
who posts one within the week is in a random draw for a prize, drawn and announced
by the site's owner on his X account one week after the day. The leaderboard is
the prize for playing; the draw is for the videos.

The short version to say out loud: "Film your run, post it with #GatekeeperJava
and @Sablenetwork, and you are in the draw."

Two things to keep straight: the video is about the game, not the token (a video
that mentions the token is out of the draw, for the same reason as the one rule),
and filming a friend needs their yes.

## What counts

- Counted: a run started from your link, 20 seconds or longer, submitted with a
  name. Runs the referee flags as scripted do not count.
- You can see the count yourself, any time:
  `https://sable.primecircle.cloud/api/game/src?tag=java` (runs, players, best).
  On the game page, a tab "from java" lists the runs.
- Photos are the story. Runs are the measure.

## Photos and content

- Ask before you photograph anyone: "Boleh saya foto untuk media sosial?" No photos
  of children. No faces of people who said no.
- Send the same evening: 10 to 20 photos, a few short vertical clips of 10 to 20
  seconds, the city and the place, and how many people played. Send by [WhatsApp or
  Telegram].
- If you post about it yourself: no token, no price, tag only the site. Say who you
  ran it with, so nobody thinks you are Sable. A caption is in appendix B. If anything
  about this day is sponsored, Indonesian rules (POJK 6/2026) require you to say so
  in your own post.

## Do not

- Do not shout anything about SABL or crypto. Shouting "come play a game" is fine.
- No stickers on public property, no posters over other people's posters, no
  blocking a sidewalk.
- Do not collect anyone's phone number, wallet address, or email. The game needs
  none of them.
- Do not promise anything beyond the board and the video draw. The draw is one
  week after the day; what the prize is, is the site owner's to announce.
- Do not argue with police or security. Pack up and move.

## Day checklist

- [ ] Evening: photos, clips, the count, sent.

## Appendix C: questions people ask

- "What is Sable?" A service where AI agents buy computing and get a signed receipt
  for every answer. The site explains it, with a simulation you can click through.
- "Is this a scam?" There is nothing to give. No wallet, no sign-up, no payment. Play
  and leave.
- "Who made the game?" A private person in the Netherlands who watches Sable from
  the outside and built the site. His X handle is on the site.
- "Can I win something?" Your name lit on the board if you are top three today.
  And if you post a video of your run with #GatekeeperJava and @Sablenetwork, you
  are in a random draw for a prize.
- "Why are you doing this?" The person who runs the site asked me to run a game day
  here. I am not from Sable.
