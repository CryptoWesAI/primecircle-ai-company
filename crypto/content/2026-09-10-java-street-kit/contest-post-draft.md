## Notes for the founder

- Window: from the event day until the same time one week later; the draw is on
  day seven. A video posted after that is not an entry.
- Entries: every X post in the window that carries #GatekeeperJava, tags
  @Sablenetwork, and shows a real run. Optional check: a matching run on the
  "from java" board. Posts that mention the token are out; say so in the
  announcement, then apply it without exception.
- A random draw is a prize draw. Indonesia licenses public prize draws (undian
  gratis berhadiah, a Ministry of Social Affairs permit) when a business runs
  one for promotion; a small personal draw run from your own account in the
  Netherlands is a different thing, but check before you scale it or let him
  run it in his name. A judged pick (best video) avoids the question entirely
  if it ever matters.
- Make the draw reproducible: a public seed and a published entry list, so the
  pick can be re-run by anyone. The draft above uses the mint supply reading.
- No prize in SABL. A token prize makes the winner a holder promoting an asset
  that is not on the OJK list.
- The 7 to 14 September contest asks players to tag @Sablenetwork with their
  card; that one stays as it is. The draw is separate.
