# Peer posts: one per project, two threads, a week of posting

Written 2026-09-09 for @0PTIMUS_ONE. Every post below is under 280 characters with the link counted as 23 (X shortens every URL to 23). Each post gives the tagged community something true and useful about their own project first, then one honest contrast, then the page. The page carries the full cards and the sources, so the post never has to argue. Every post ends with the disclosure.

## How this funnels without spamming

- One project per post, one post per half day. Thirteen tags in one post reads as a raid; thirteen posts over a week reads as a series. Post the launch thread first, the singles over six days, the Sable thread last.
- Media on every post: the card image for that project (`sites/sable-peers/tests/shots/cards/<slug>.png`, 2x). A card with dates and sources is what makes the tag welcome rather than noise.
- Reply, do not broadcast. When the tagged team or its members answer, answer with the source (the fact sheet has every URL). If a fact is wrong, fix the card the same day and say so; the corrections list on the page is for that.
- The token stays out of the copy except the disclosure. "I hold SABL." is the last sentence of every post: MiCA needs it, and it is the reason a reader believes the rest.
- Never post into a project's Telegram or Discord as an announcement. Post the finding as a member, when the topic comes up, with the link.
- Do not post the Secret one on the day of bad news for them. It is written with sympathy; timing decides how it reads.

## Thread 2: what would move Sable up the ladder (post last, tag Sable)

1/ I compared @Sablenetwork with twelve tokens and one startup, from their own docs and CoinGecko on 9 Sep 2026. Here is what would move Sable up the ladder, in the order it explains the distance. Not hopes, measurements. A thread.

2/ Market. Every ladder token above $20M trades on Binance, Coinbase, Kraken or Upbit with millions a day. SABL trades on one Solana pool with about $60K of liquidity. Until a market with depth exists, nothing else can move the bar by more than the pool allows.

3/ GPU TEE. @PhalaNetwork, @near_ai, @nillion, Secret and @TinfoilAI run models on GPU TEEs; @akashnet joined in July. Sable serves two small CPU-only models on TDX, and that tier has refused every request since 4 September. Capability and reliability are one conversation.

4/ Partners. @near_ai named Brave, OpenMind and Phala on day one. Sable has an OpenRouter application and no named integration. One partner routing real traffic through the gateway would say more than any feature.

5/ One pitch. Phala's three repositionings and Marlin's quiet years show the cost of drift; Akash kept one line and it paid off when the AI wave arrived. "Compute you can prove" is a line worth keeping, version labels included.

6/ Token role. @OpenGradient and @nillion launched token utility before fee-paying usage and fell 79 and 94 percent. Sable's order, USDT first and SABL optional, is right. So the cap has no reason to move before the pay-in is live and the burn is on-chain. I watch that hourly.

7/ Reliability. Secret's 2026 shows what improvising after attestation breaks costs. Failing closed is right; five days closed without a note is not. Phala, @virtuals_io and Akash moved builders by paying for checkable output. A small grants line would be the cheapest signal.

8/ The full list, the cards, the sources: sable.primecircle.cloud/#who If Sable's team disagrees with a line, the page has a corrections list and I use it. Not affiliated. I hold SABL.
