# The artist base

Paste the founder's Suno artist description below the line. It becomes the
first half of every track's Style field (the second half is the track's mood
line). Keep the whole Style field under 1,000 characters, so the base should
stay under about 600.

What a good base names: the voice (range, gender, grain, spoken or sung),
two or three lead instruments, the era or scene it sits in, the tempo range,
the production feel (dry, wide, lo-fi, polished), and one thing it never does.
Genre words help Suno more than adjectives.

Also fill in: the Suno artist (Voices) name as it appears in the UI, and the
model used, so a retake months later can match.
