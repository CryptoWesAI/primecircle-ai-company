# Oasis Network (ROSE): Fact Sheet

## 1. What it is
Oasis is a privacy-focused Layer 1 blockchain combining confidential smart contracts with off-chain trusted-execution compute, for DeFi and AI. Oasis Labs, the founding contributor, was founded 2018 by Dawn Song (UC Berkeley professor, MacArthur Fellow), with Noah Johnson, Bobby Jaros, Raymond Cheng. Governance sits with the separate Oasis Protocol Foundation (Switzerland-based per project materials, not independently confirmed). Funding: $45 million private pre-sale closed July 9, 2018, led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Binance, Accel, DCVC, Electric Capital, Pantera, and Polychain (Forbes, CCN, July 2018). Tracxn: $48 million total, 3 rounds, 51 investors, latest Series B November 23, 2020 (round amount: not found).

## 2. Product: ROFL and confidential compute
ROFL (Runtime Off-chain Logic) runs apps inside Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs), anchored to Oasis Sapphire. Per docs.oasis.io, ROFL apps run as "Docker-like containers or single-executable apps depending on your TCB demand and threat model," delivering "Privacy and integrity through Intel SGX/TDX including fully auditable history of updates," with a "Built-in Key Management Service (KMS) for app secrets and secure key derivation within TEE." The docs state ROFL "powers private trading and chat bots, provable AI learning, price oracles, home automation, VPNs and fair gaming." Sapphire has run Solidity inside Intel SGX since 2020. ROFL reached mainnet July 2, 2025 (Oasis Foundation, Chainwire), for AI inference, oracles, and agents.

## 3. Token
Ticker: ROSE, on Oasis's own Layer 1 (historically also wrapped on Ethereum/Binance Chain). Mainnet, first transferability: November 18, 2020, after Mainnet Beta, October 1, 2020. Supply: fixed max/total 10 billion ROSE, circulating supply at launch about 1.5 billion; about 2.3 billion earmarked for staking rewards over time (docs.oasis.io token-metrics page). Circulating supply 2026-09-09: approximately 8.04 billion (CoinGecko). Utility: fees, staking, delegation, governance, plus compute fees. First major listing: Binance, 08:00 UTC, November 19, 2020.

## 4. Market cap arc
All-time high: $0.5973 on January 14, 2022 (CoinGecko, accessed 2026-09-09). Current: market cap approximately $55.36 million, price approximately $0.006887, rank roughly #428 (CoinGecko, accessed 2026-09-09). The 2022 peak coincided with Oasis's DeFi push: YuzuSwap DEX and Wormhole bridge went live around January 11, 2022, plus Binance Labs adding to the Ecosystem Fund. ROSE fell roughly 87% from January 2022 to January 2025. Cited factors: scheduled unlocks (~200 million ROSE, ~$14 million cliff unlock, November 2023), the 2022-2023 bear market, fading DeFi momentum. Inference: price sits far below ATH mainly because the 2022 narrative did not sustain, while the 2025 ROFL/AI narrative has not produced a comparable move.

## 5. Community

Telegram: not found. Programs: an Ecosystem Fund (Binance Labs, January 2022), hackathons/grants referenced on oasis.net and Medium; scale not verified further.

## 6. Connection to Sable Network
Overlap: both sell trust infrastructure for AI compute using TEEs (Oasis: SGX/TDX; Sable: TDX) with cryptographic verification. Difference: Oasis is a full Layer 1 with its own token and validators, offering confidential compute alongside DeFi and gaming; Sable is a narrower pay-as-you-go gateway with sealed prompts, spend caps, and signed receipts, settling via USDT or x402, SABL optional, no chain of its own. What Sable could learn: Oasis shipped confidential EVM compute (2020) years before its AI-specific ROFL mainnet (2025), showing credibility builds incrementally, but its price history shows shipping infra alone did not sustain valuation once the DeFi narrative faded. Inference: an observer would still list Oasis as a Sable peer, despite Oasis's full L1 versus Sable's lighter gateway, since both frame themselves as the TEE-attested trust layer for agentic compute.
