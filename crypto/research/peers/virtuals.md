# Virtuals Protocol (VIRTUAL) — Project Fact Sheet

Compiled 2026-09-09. All figures dated; crypto data moves by the minute.

## 1. What it is

An AI agent launchpad and "agent commerce protocol": anyone can create, tokenize, and co-own autonomous agents that transact onchain, mainly on Base. Started 2021 as PathDAO (gaming guild), pivoted to AI agents January 2024, later rebranded. Founders per aggregators, not a primary source: Jansen Teng and Wee Kee Tiew (Tracxn, CB Insights); HQ listed as Kuala Lumpur. Funding: $16M seed (2021) led by DeFiance Capital and Beam plus smaller funds, unverified against a primary release.

## 2. Product

Docs: "Virtuals Protocol is a society of AI agents... autonomous agents have identity, capital, jobs, markets, governance, and, increasingly, bodies in the physical world" (whitepaper.virtuals.io). **Agent tokenization** is "the capital markets layer... lets founders compose the right capital structure... from independent primitives" (docs): commit 100 VIRTUAL to a bonding curve for an agent token; the curve fills into a pool against VIRTUAL, and holders get governance plus revenue share. **ACP** is "the commerce layer... [for] secure, transparent, and verifiable commerce between autonomous AI agents" (docs); built February 2025, public beta from July 3 (year unconfirmed). After "18 months in production and over 2,000 agents onboarded," ACP v2.0 shipped (hook-based, multi-chain, non-custodial wallet; exact date not found).

## 3. Token

VIRTUAL, native to Base, also on Ethereum and Solana. Fixed supply: 1B total/max, ~658-659M circulating (CoinGecko, CMC). Utility: required to launch agents, base trading pair for agent tokens, target of revenue-funded buybacks/burns (deflationary, per CoinGecko). All-time low $0.0075, January 23, 2024, implying DEX trading by then; first CEX listing date not found (a claimed December 2023 Binance futures listing predates the apparent debut, so not reported as fact). Confirmed later listings: OKX spot (Oct 2025), Coinbase spot (Apr 2026), eToro (May 2026); trades on several other major exchanges too, first dates unconfirmed.

## 4. Market cap arc

ATH price $5.07-5.12, Jan 1-2, 2025 (CoinGecko, CMC, CoinLore). ATH market cap is reported inconsistently: $2.25B (Jan 10, post-pullback) up to $3.24-3.3B circulating and ~$4.6-5B fully diluted; my own inference (not sourced fact) from ATH price and plausible supply is ~$3.0-3.3B at peak. The peak rode the AI-agent narrative of late 2024/early 2025, when AI tokens as a category reportedly hit $61.5B combined cap by mid-December 2024 (Investorideas.com). Current: market cap ~$469-470M, price ~$0.71-0.72, 2026-09-09 (CoinGecko, CMC), ~85-86% below peak, attributed by CoinJournal/Yellow.com to slowing agent creation and revenue down ~99% from peak as speculative capital left in 2025; not independently verified.

## 5. Community

X: ~289,000-294,000 followers (CertiK, Binance Square). Telegram: 25,000+; Discord: 7,600+ (both undated). GitHub: 373 followers, 400+ stars. On-chain holders: ~1.12M addresses (CMC), a wallet count, not engagement. Programs: Virtuals Partners Network (Mar 2025), grants to $50,000; a "Revenue Incentives" program reportedly paid ~$500,000 to 1,500+ revenue-generating agents in its first two-week epoch (@virtuals_io, undated). Talk happens mainly on X, then Telegram/Discord, GitHub for builders; some agent accounts (aixbt) out-follow the main project.

## 6. Connection to Sable Network

Virtuals agents need metered compute and machine-to-machine payment, gaps Sable targets: as a pay-as-you-go gateway behind ACP agents, as settlement for micropayments ACP coordinates but doesn't provide, and as receipts proving to co-owners a paid call happened. Genuinely different: Virtuals sells agent identity and commerce, not verifiable compute. Lesson for Sable: revenue-tied builder payouts and a repeated hackathon cadence moved builders; growth on self-reported counts didn't hold once speculative attention left. An observer lists Virtuals as a peer since both sit where "AI agents need money and machines to act," Virtuals owning commerce, Sable the plumbing underneath.
