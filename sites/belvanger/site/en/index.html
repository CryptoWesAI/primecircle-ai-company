One more thing

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. It works on your own number. We set up a divert so a missed call reaches us. You won't notice a thing, except the leads rolling in.

Does my customer need to install an app?

No. Your customer just gets a message on their phone, like from a friend. Nothing to download, nothing complicated.

I already have a website. What then?

Then we rebuild it, and better. We don't bolt the lead capture onto someone else's site: that leaves you depending on their hosting, updates and schedule. Everything in one hand is faster and breaks less often. You keep your own domain.

Am I tied to anything?

No. Cancel monthly. Not working for you? You just stop.

Does this work with the way I work?

Yes. You keep calling back yourself. We just make sure you don't lose the customer. No robot taking over your work, just a safety net underneath.

What does it cost?

Two parts: a one-time setup from €625 as a founding partner (normally €1,250), building your website, AI chatbot and the automation, and then €99 per month for running it, maintenance and your dashboard with daily figures. The exact amount depends on your wishes; we discuss that in a no-obligation call. Cancel monthly. One extra job a month more than pays for it.

What do you do with the data?

We only store what's needed to call back: the phone number and what the job's about. We don't sell anything on, and data can be deleted on request.
